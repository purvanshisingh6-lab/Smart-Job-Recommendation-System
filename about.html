About SmartHire

Smart Job Recommendation and Skill Matching System

Project description

SmartHire helps students find the roles they are closest to qualified for. A student selects skills, and the system ranks job roles by the percentage of required skills they already have, showing matched skills in green and missing skills in red for every role.

Match score

Match percentage = (matched required skills ÷ total required skills) × 100. Roles with no matching skills are excluded and remaining roles are ranked by percentage.
